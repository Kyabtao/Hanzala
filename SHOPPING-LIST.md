# Ḥanzalā — Shopping List (products like the Meesho "First Words" set, age by age)

**The linked product:** *Kids First Words Board Book Collection – 6 Books for Preschool Learning.*
It is 6 small board books of pictures + first words. A good idea — and mostly **free to make**: this repo's
**`printable/books.html`** prints **8 books · 48 word pages** (Arabic + Urdu + English), plus `printable/cards.html`
(56 flashcards). The shop set has 6 books; ours has 8, and ours speak three languages.

**Buy vs print, in one line:** buy what must survive chewing, water and throwing (wood, cloth, silicone);
**print** everything that is just pictures and words.

---

## 0. Five rules before any order

1. **Wishlist, don't impulse-buy.** "25% off first order" and "new products every 3 hours" are timers, not needs. Fill the wishlist, wait 24 h, then buy what is still wanted.
2. **Check the age label on every product.** If it says 3+, and he is 0–2, it is a choking hazard, not a bargain.
3. **No button batteries, no strong smell, no soft PVC.** (Rule 3 of the main plan — ḍarar is removed.)
4. **One search term beats one product link.** Product links die; search terms work forever on any app (Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart, local market).
5. **70% of the list can be second-hand or DIY** — spend on blocks, books, the Qāʿidah, the bike.

## 5. Never buy — even at 70% off

- **Walkers** (pediatricians advise against; delays walking) · activity centres that lock him in
- **Anything with button batteries or small high-power magnets** (burns/intestines — real ER cases)
- **Light-and-sound plastic** that performs *for* him instead of him doing the work
- **"Educational tablet / flash-card app" for 0–2** — screens before 18–24 months, none, at any discount
- **Big plush in the cot** (smothering risk) · strong-smelling soft PVC
- Stories/pictures that normalize what Allāh called wrong — the plan's avoid-list applies to toys too

**After buying:** log it on the tracker's *yearly record* page, and rotate — 5–8 toys out, the rest hidden.
The wishlist trick that actually saves money: write the item + price in `updates/` (this repo's notes folder) with today's
date, check again in a week — the timer pressure will be gone and you will know if you truly want it.
