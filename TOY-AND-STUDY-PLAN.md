# Hanzala — Toy & Study Plan (with a Muslim angle)

**Child's date of birth:** 11 January 2026
**Made:** 5 September 2026 — child is **7 months old**
**How to use:** Work on the *current* age section only. Read the next section 1 month early so you are ready. Revisit every birthday. Everything here is **optional** — the sunnah is play and duʿāʾ, not buying things.

---

**Weekly schedule:** `WEEKLY-PLAN-YEAR1.md` — 52 weeks, Sep 2026 → Aug 2027 (āyah of the week, words, toys, Islamic dates)

## 0. The 6 rules that matter more than any toy

1. **Your voice beats any product.** Reciting Qurʾān aloud in the house, dhikr while cooking, duʿāʾ before sleep — this is the #1 "Islamic toy" and it costs nothing.
2. **Halāl & ṭayyib materials.** Choose wood, cotton, silicone, bamboo. Avoid strong chemical smell, soft PVC, and cheap batteries. Better for ʿaqīdah of the body (amānah) and for the dūnyā.
3. **Safety first — this is also dīn.** Harm (ḍarar) is forbidden. Under 3 years: no button batteries, no high-powered magnets, nothing that fits inside a toilet-paper roll (it fits in a throat).
4. **Fewer toys, more attention.** 5–8 toys out, rest hidden, rotate weekly. Clutter shortens attention span; a "toy rotation" feels like ʿīd every week.
5. **No screens before 18–24 months.** After that: max 15–20 min/day, only with you sitting beside him, never at suḥūr/iftār-style "eating time" and never 1 hour before sleep.
6. **Play is sunnah.** The Prophet ﷺ played with children, raced, carried his grandchildren on his back. Do not feel guilty about playing — it is ṣadaqah to your child.

## 1. Ages 0–12 months *(now → 11 Jan 2027)*

### What his brain needs
Grasp → roll → sit → crawl → pull up. Learning = mouth + hands + ears.

### Buy list (only these)
| # | Toy | Why | Age |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Black/white/red high-contrast cards or book | Newborn vision is built for contrast; calms and focuses | 0–4 m |
| 2 | Soft fabric mirror (unbreakable) | First "face" game, self-recognition | 2 m+ |
| 3 | Crinkle cloth books (2–4) | Chewing + turning pages = hand-eye | 3 m+ |
| 4 | Wooden/ silicone rattles (2–3) | Cause & effect, grip strength | 3–6 m |
| 5 | Teething: silicone ring, chilled (not frozen) cloth | Saves you both at 5–8 m | 4 m+ |
| 6 | Play mat + soft gym, or just a clean blanket on the floor | Tummy time is the single best "exercise" | 0–12 m |
| 7 | Soft ball (crinkle, 15 cm) | Rolling game = turn-taking, the first social skill | 6 m+ |
| 8 | Stacking cups (3–4, big) or wooden rings | Cup in cup = the first "problem solved" | 8 m+ |
| 9 | Simple busy board with 1 latch + 1 velcro | Finger dexterity, curiosity without danger | 9–12 m |
| 10 | Bath toys: 3–4 cups, no holes (mould-free) | Water = sunnah of ṭahārah, clean fun | 6 m+ |

### Skip / waste of money
Activity centres that lock him in, walker (banned by pediatricians, harms ṣibāh/legs), plush toys bigger than his head (sleep risk), anything with lights + sounds that he watches *instead of* doing the work, "educational" flash-card apps for babies.

### Muslim-angle activities (10 min each, 3–4× a day)
- **Talqīn of the sounds:** Recite **Aʿdān al-Ṣalāh / Sūrah al-Aʿlā / Yāsīn** softly near his ear after each ṣalāh. Babies learn the *music* of Arabic before the meaning — this is why ḥuffāẓ started as infants.
- **Kalimah loop:** Say **"Lā ilāha illallāh, Muḥammadur Rasūlullāh"** slowly 10× while rocking him. Repetition is the baby's whole curriculum.
- **Āyah of the week:** one short sūrah (al-Fātiḥah, al-Ikhlāṣ, al-Falaq, an-Nās). You repeat it all day for every situation — while changing him, feeding, walking him to sleep.
- **Duʿāʾ of protection** on him each morning/evening: *Aʿūdhu bi-kalimāti Llāhi t-tāmmāti min kulli shayṭānin wa hāmmatin wa min kulli ʿaynin lāmmah.* (Abū Dāwūd) — wipe his head, duʿāʾ for ḥifẓ.
- **Naming duʿāʾ:** every time you call "Yā Ḥanzalā!" you are making duʿāʾ for him — call his name warmly, often, from across the room.
- **Ṣadaqah habit:** give him one biscuit/fruit piece and say "Bismillāh" — he can't do it yet, but the word becomes linked to food forever.
- **Sensational ḥalāl play:** roll the crinkle ball and say "Allāhu akbar!" when he catches it — praise with dhikr, not just "good boy."

### Daily rhythm template (7 months)
| Time | What | Muslim angle |
|---|---|---|
| Wake | Change, feed, open curtains | "Alḥamdulillāhi lladhī aḥyānā baʿda mā amātanā" |
| After Fajr | Floor play / tummy time + āyah of the week | Quiet brain hour — best for listening |
| Mid-morning | Outside walk, touch leaves, water, soil | "Tafakkur" — point and say "SubḥānAllāh" |
| Post-Ẓuhr | Nap + crinkle book | Play Sūrah softly, don't force sleep association |
| Afternoon | Stacking cups / busy board / crawling obstacle | Say "Bismillāh" before play, "Alḥamdulillāh" after |
| Maghrib | Family time, no screens | He sits in the majlis for 5 min — belonging |
| ʿIshāʾ | Bath → 3 min massage → bed | Duʿāʾ sleep + right side + 3 Qulūth |

## 2. Ages 12–24 months *(11 Jan 2027 → 11 Jan 2028)*

### Buy list
- **Wooden blocks, 40–60 pieces** (the single highest-value toy of childhood)
- **Push-pull wagon / trolley** (not a walker — he is already walking)
- **Large soft building blocks / card-box bricks** — build & destroy, he'll love it
- **Shape sorter** (circle, square, triangle only at first)
- **Stacking ring, 6 discs** + **nesting boxes**
- **Play-dough (flour + salt + oil + water recipe — you make it, so it's ḥalāl and safe if eaten)**
- **Toy knife + soft fruits/veg for cutting** (Velcro fruit)
- **Mini broom, small towel, jug** — *chores as toys*, deeply Islamic
- **Musical: wooden shaker, small drum, bells** (rhythmic, keep it acoustic)
- **Books — 10 favourites, hardboard:** animals, vehicles, body parts, bedtime, Muslims-pray style board books
- **Chunky crayons (triangular) + paper taped to the wall**
- **Sand/water play: 2 buckets + 1 jug + spoons** (outdoor, best 20-min time-killer)
- **Doll + small blanket + cup** (for mercy-training — see below)
- **Puzzle: 3–6 piece knob puzzles, wooden**
- **Ride-on (small, feet on floor) + helmet from 18 m**
- **First prayer-rug the right size** (his own, ~90 cm)

### Skip
Electronic "teaches ABC 123" tablets, licensed-character toys at this age, anything with small removable parts, trampolines, toy weapons with sharp edges.

### Muslim-angle activities
- **Pray *at* him, not *at* the TV:** lay his rug, stand him beside you, put his little forehead down when you say sujud. Copying prayer is the learning method of this age — do not correct, do not interrupt.
- **Adhkār as games:**
  - Bismillāh → before food, before play, before getting in the car
  - Alḥamdulillāh → after eating, after sneezing, after a finished tower
  - Allāhu akbar → when he sees something big (dog, plane, mountain, your prayer)
  - SubḥānAllāh → the sky, the moon, the baby cousin, rain
  - Āmīn → after any duʿāʾ you make out loud for him
- **Aḍḥáḥá (mercy) training:** he must be gentle with the doll, the cat, the ants. Hadith: *"The one who is not merciful to our young is not of us."* When he hits the doll, don't shout — take his hand and stroke the doll: "Be soft, inshāʾAllāh."
- **Sharākah (sharing) games:** 2 biscuits, "one for you, one for Ummī"; the toy must be given back. Practise now, no lecture later.
- **Tahārah play:** let him pour water over your hands, wash his own feet before bed. "Cleanliness is half of faith" becomes muscle memory.
- **Ṣadaqah box at home:** a small tin; each week he puts in one coin (or one fruit for the neighbour). Say duʿāʾ when he gives.
- **Names of Allāh, 1 per month:** ar-Raḥmān, al-Wāḥid, as-Samīʿ, al-Bāṣir, al-Khāliq… Say it 10× while pointing at what it means ("Allāh is al-Bāṣir — He sees your little car under the chair!").
- **First "study":** 5 min, 2×/day. Only this: 4 letters (ا ب ت ث or his own name letters), 1 sūrah, 1 animal sound, 1 colour. Sit with him, do not test him.

## 3. Ages 2–3 years *(11 Jan 2028 → 11 Jan 2029)*

### Buy list
- **Duplo-style big bricks** + **wooden train set + track** (the two toys with the longest play-life)
- **Puzzles 10–20 pieces**, **magnetic wooden puzzle letters**
- **Dress-up:** thobe/kurta + shawl, firefighter, doctor, animal masks (identity play, empathy)
- **Play kitchen + dishes + real small jug** (he serves you; adab of serving)
- **Balance bike** (better than a tricycle; helmet always) + **sandbox set**
- **Art station:** washable poster paint, chunky brushes, 3 sizes of paper, glue stick, safety scissors
- **Play-dough tools + rolling pin + real cookie cutters**
- **Marble run / wooden ramp cars** (early physics, no battery)
- **Board games of the simple kind:** first cooperative games, dominoes, memory card pairs (animals)
- **A "little library" of 25 books:** prophets' stories for small ones, ḥadīth-of-the-week style, emotions books, nature books, Arabic alphabet books
- **Qurbāni play set (pretend animals + no knife)** — for ʿĪd understanding
- **Hajj sandbox play:** sand, small stones, boxes = kaʿbah, ʿarafah tent (build it together on Dhū al-Ḥijjah 9)
- **First "own" things**: own miswāk/toothbrush, own small towel, own prayer rug, own bag

### Muslim-angle activities
- **Ṣalāh with ṭumaʾnīnah:** start teaching the *positions*, one per week. Use a chart with pictures on the wall. Never force more than 2 rakʿahs with you; "who finishes with Ummī gets a hug + date."
- **Sūrah progress:** al-Mulk + as-Sajdah at night (sunnah before sleep) + Juz ʿAmma short sūrahs. Target: **15 short sūrahs by age 3** (al-Fātiḥah → aḍ-Ḍuḥā).
- **Ruwā of the prophets (child-safe):** 3 min each night — Ādam, Nūḥ (the ship!), Ibrāhīm (the fire that became a garden), Yūsuf, Mūsā (the sea), Yūnus (the whale), ʿĪsā, Muḥammad ﷺ (his birth, his kindness to children). Retell orally, do not skip; do not draw the prophets.
- **First fast, symbolically:** half day on ʿĀshūrāʾ and the white days, plus Ramadān "until Ẓuhr." Give a plate for suḥūr — the memory matters more than the hunger.
- **Two ʿīds as projects:** 3 days before, make a card, choose the clothes, give ṣadaqah, visit the neighbour. Write a "ʿīd checklist" with his photo.
- **Adab curriculum (behaviour, not knowledge):** sneezing duʿāʾ, knocking 3× and saying salām before entering, not eating left-handed, saying "Ummī/Abū" kindly, no shouting in the house, greeting the neighbours, kissing grandparents' hands.
- **Qiyām of the heart:** wake him 5 min before Fajr once a week, make duʿāʾ with his hands up, one date, then back to bed. "Yā Allāh, help Ḥanzalā."
- **Nature as a madrasah:** monthly trip — sea/river, farm, mountain, market. Rule: he must bring back 1 leaf/1 stone and you ask "who made this?" (al-Khāliq).
- **Animal mercy day:** feed birds, put out water for insects; the woman in the ḥadīth who was punished for a cat, and the man forgiven for giving a thirsty dog water — tell both stories at 2.5+.
- **Study plan, 10–15 min × 2/day:** hijāʾ letters (all 28 by 3.5), write in sand/flour/tray (no pencils yet), count to 20 with beads, sort by colour/shape, name body parts in Arabic, one new English word per day with you speaking it (not an app).

## 4. Ages 3–5 years *(11 Jan 2029 → 11 Jan 2031)* — the golden years of memory

### Buy list
- **Qurʾān: one mushaf for the house + one kid-friendly Qāʿidah** (Nūrānī Qāʿidah / al-Baghī / Madinī Primer / Qāʿidah al-Fātiḥah — pick ONE and stick with it)
- **Arabic letter magnets + sand tray for handwriting**
- **Blocks: wooden unit blocks, Lego/Duplo, magnets (as a set, not hundreds of pieces)**
- **Real tools play: small hammer + wooden planks + safety goggles, screwdriver set** (Amānah on hands; also builds focus)
- **Jigsaw 40–80 pieces**, **Sudoku for kids**, **pattern blocks**
- **Sewing/cards: laminated āyah-matching cards, "build a word" cards**
- **Musical: oud/qanun *or* a real beginner percussion (duff/ṭar only, keep it simple)**, plus a recorder if you want an instrument
- **Bicycle with pedals at 4**, swimming lessons at 4 (sunnah skill: swimming, archery-style target games, running races)
- **Board games:** snakes & ladders, ludo, Checkers/English draughts (kids version), Story Cubes, Blokus junior
- **Science-lite toys:** kids microscope, magnets, bug box, water filter/sprouting kit, dominoes
- **World play:** prayer beads (his own misbaḥah, 33), hijab/prayer set for family, small kaʿbah model, Makkah/Madinah picture book
- **A "work desk" of his own**, 30 min of sitting, 1 chair, 1 lamp — the desk is the respect for learning

### Muslim-angle activities
- **The 5-per-day structure, not the count:** he prays *with* the family, and you ask afterwards "what did you say to Allāh?" Not a quiz — a chat.
- **Qurʾān ḥalqah at home:** after Fajr or Maghrib, 20 min, sit with the mushaf, listen first, then repeat. Do not rush reading — correct pronunciation *now* is a gift for life.
- **Ḥifẓ target (realistic, no pressure):** Juz ʿAmma by 5, Sūrah al-Mulk by 5.5. 5 new āyāt a day, revise 3 days. If he resists for 2 weeks, stop and restart — never make Qurʾān a punishment; that is how children grow up hating it.
- **40 ḥadīths of the small:** An-Nawawī's 42→ choose 10 for the year: actions by intentions, cleanliness is half of faith, be not angry, smile is ṣadaqah, feed the hungry, seek knowledge, honour your mother, no harm, good character, duʿāʾ.
- **Aḥl al-Bayt & ṣaḥābah as friends:** tell stories of Fāṭimah az-Zahrāʾ, al-Ḥasan & al-Ḥusayn, Bilāl, Khadījah, ʿUmar's justice, Ṣalāḥ ad-Dīn's kindness — one each Friday after ʿIshāʾ, 5 min.
- **Friday ritual (Jumuʿah):** bath + thobe + smell good + go to the musalla (even if he plays outside the row) + one sūrah al-Kahf ayah read by Abū + give coins in the box + lunch is special. This single routine creates the identity of a Muslim man/boy more than any lesson.
- **Ramadān plan for 4–5:** suḥūr with the family, one ṣawm a week, help lay the table for iftār, count the misbaḥah after tarāwīḥ, "Quran khatm chart" with stars, last 10 nights — wake for Qadr once, and let him "watch" the duʿāʾ.
- **Charity with his own hands:** choose 1 of his toys/clothes and give them; take him to distribute a meal once per Ramadān. Teach "the hand that gives."
- **Guardianship of speech (ḥifẓ al-lisān):** no swearing at home, no "I hate", no lying even about a broken cup — praise truth-telling *after* a mistake: "You told me the truth — that is the best thing, now we clean it together."
- **Screen rule, hard line:** only after Qurʾān and homework; max 30 min/day; 1 shared show per week with the family (e.g. a Prophets-style animated series) and you discuss it; no device in the bedroom, no device at meals, no device before Fajr. Use a filter/router schedule — parents control the wifi.
- **Study blocks (30–40 min, split):** Arabic letters + Qurʾān 15, numbers 20/100/counting objects 10, English phonics (read to him nightly) 10, one worksheet 5. Everything with a *physical* object in the hand.

## 5. Ages 6–10 (school years) — quick forward map
- **Skills to buy, not toys:** swimming (must), archery/target & fencing-style games, cycling, horse/pool/skate, one team sport, first *real* responsibility: his own lunch box, his own bag, wake himself for Fajr.
- **Islamic syllabus by age 10:** ʿaqīdah of the 6 imān (in his own words), fiqh of ṭahārah & ṣalāh & fasting & ḥajj (do ʿumrah with him — the best "field trip" in Islam), sīrah of the Prophet ﷺ cover to cover in child books, 20 chosen duʿāʾ of morning/evening, sūrah al-Baqarah recitation progress, Arabic grammar starting with al-Ājurrūmiyyah-lite, one *seerah biography* read per month.
- **One weekly "dars" with Abū:** Thursday after Maghrib, 45 min, tea + biscuits, 1 ḥadīth + 1 real-life challenge for the week ("this week, no anger").
- **Money & ḥalāl:** give a small weekly allowance, 3 jars: spend / ṣadaqah / save. No interest talk needed yet, but "we don't buy games with the ṣadaqah jar."
- **Friendship filter:** know the names of 5 friends and their parents; host them at home (you can see what they watch). "A man is on the dīn of his friend."
- **Teach him to *make* things:** sewing a button, tying a knot, fixing a bike, cooking 3 dishes by 10, using a knife, cleaning after himself. Competence is ʿizzah.

## 6. What to AVOID — dīn and safety reasons
| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Toys with shirk symbols (crosses as decoration, statues to venerate, magic/wizard "power" games, tarot-style cards, evil-eye amulets as toy accessories) | Protection of tawḥīd — the mind at 3–7 accepts pictures as truth |
| "God is a baby", "Jesus plays football" style books, and any story that says Allāh has a son | Fix ʿaqīdah before story-time |
| Music-heavy electronic toys that replace talking; loud sound toys (>85 dB harms infant hearing) | Hearing is amānah; speech delay is real |
| Toy guns/realistic weapons, cartoon characters known for vulgarity | Keep it clean |
| Music/singing — the scholarly position differs, so the safe path: **duʿāʾ, nasheeds without instruments, ṭar/duff at ʿīd and weddings, Qurʾān, poems of ḥikmah.** Be moderate, don't make the home silent like a graveyard, and don't fill it with loud clubs | Ijtihād area — follow your madhhab/scholar and be balanced |
| Anime/cartoons with sexualised characters, or the "free apps" full of ads | Lower the gaze, protect the mind; parents filter |
| Music/beats while sleeping, videos as a babysitter | Sleep & attention are sunnah rights |
| Gold/silk for him as a boy later (and anything imitating the opposite sex in play) | Fiqh of clothing, teach naturally at 5–6 |
| Dogs as indoor pets, pig-themed toys | Najāsah/ḥukm — explain simply, no shaming |
| Halloween, birthday "cake worship", Christmas toys, Valentine's stuff, magic trick "spells" | Muslim identity — teach him he has two ʿīds of his own |
| Small batteries, magnets, marbles under age 3; button batteries = emergency | Ḍarar is forbidden; keep them out of the house |
| Unrestricted YouTube | Nothing helps a 4-year-old more than the removal of autoplay |

## 9. Milestone checklists — if 2+ items miss in one age, see your pediatrician

**Development (WHO/AAP style):**
- **6 m:** sits with support · laughs · reaches for toys · turns to his name · babbles "ba da"
- **12 m:** pulls to stand · 1–3 words · points with finger · waves bye · crawls/cruises · looks where you point
- **18 m:** walks well · ~10–20 words · 2-block tower · pretends with a doll · follows 1-step instruction
- **24 m:** 2-word phrases · ~50+ words · runs, kicks · parallel play · removes socks/shoes
- **36 m:** 3–4-word sentences · jumps with both feet · turns pages · asks "what's that?" · copies a circle
- **48 m:** tells a short story · hops on one foot · copies a cross/square · counts 4 objects · washes hands
- **60 m:** knows some letters · ties knots with string · counts 10 · takes turns in a game · recites short sūrahs

**Muslim milestones (a nice, gentle chart):**
- **1 y:** responds to "Assalāmu ʿalaykum" greeting · calms when the āyah voice is heard
- **2 y:** says "Allāh" / "duʿāʾ" · makes a shape of sujud · says Bismillāh when reminded
- **3 y:** finishes "Alḥamdulillāhi…" after a sneeze · points at the sky and says "Allāh" · loves the adhān
- **4 y:** recites al-Fātiḥah with you · knows Makkah is the kaʿbah · asks "why do we fast?"
- **5 y:** stands in ṣalāh for the full positions · memorises 5+ short sūrahs · knows the 5 daily ṣalāh names · reads hijāʾ letters
- **6 y:** recites al-Fātiḥah correctly, knows the meaning of Kalimat at-Tawḥīd
- **7 y:** asked to pray (the age of the command in the sunnah) · begins regular Qurʾān lessons
- **9–10 y:** prays 5 on his own without being told · can name 6 imān · fasts Ramadān fully when able

## 10. The duʿāʾ to keep for him (write these on the fridge)
1. *Rabbi-jʿalnī muqīma ṣ-ṣalāti wa min dhurriyyatī, Rabbanā wa taqabbal duʿāʾ.* (Ibrāhīm 14:40)
2. *Rabbanā hab lanā min azwājinā wa dhurriyyātinā qurrata aʿyunin wajʿalnā lil-muttaqīna imāmā.* (al-Furqān 25:74)
3. *Allāhumma innī asʾaluka ʿilman nāfiʿan wa rizqan ṭayyiban wa ʿamalan mutaqabbalā.*
4. *Allāhumma ḥaffiẓ Ḥanzalā wa ijʿalhu qurrata ʿaynin lī, ḥāfidhan li-kitābika, ʿāmilan bi-sunnati nabiyyika.* (your own — make it in your language too; Allāh understands all)
5. *Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika an yatakhabbalani sh-shayṭānu ʿinda awājinī…* — protection duʿāʾ for children (recite over him, as the Prophet ﷺ did for al-Ḥasan and al-Ḥusayn)

### Final word
The best toy is **Abū on the floor for 20 minutes** and **Ummī's voice reciting at night**. The best "study list" is **one sūrah, one ḥadīth, one kind act — every week, for ten years.** Buy less, be present more, make duʿāʾ constantly, and Allāh is the One who grants righteous offspring — *"Lā ilāha illā Anta subḥānaka innī kuntu mina ẓ-ẓālimīn"* — and He changes hearts. May Allāh make Ḥanzala the coolness of your eyes, protect him, and make him among the ahl al-Qurʾān. Āmīn.
