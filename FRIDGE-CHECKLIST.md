## THE 6 THINGS THAT ACTUALLY RAISE A MUSLIM CHILD
1. Parents praying visibly, every day, at home
2. Qurʾān heard, not just taught
3. Duʿāʾ for him by name, often, especially at tahajjud
4. Mercy in the house — no shouting, no hitting, no sarcasm
5. Two ʿīds, Jumuʿah, Ramadān, family visits = the year's rhythm
6. Guarded eyes and ears: parent-controlled wifi, no autoplay, no unsupervised YouTube
