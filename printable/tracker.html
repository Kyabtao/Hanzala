How to use: look at this page once a month, on the 5th. Tick what he can do reliably (3 good days = yes), write the date. Never tick to feel good, and never teach to the tick — a milestone that comes late but with love beats one forced early. Ages overlap: a 14-month-old may still legitimately show 10-month ticks.

1 · Developmental milestones (WHO/AAP-style) — one row per age band

Show the doctor this month if, at 18 months: no words at all, no pointing with the index finger, no single-step understanding, loss of a skill he had, no eye contact during feeding, or he doesn't walk without falling repeatedly. Also: any regression at any age = an appointment, not a wait. Speech & motor help works best at 18–30 months. Do not use "he'll grow out of it" and do not blame yourself.

2 · Dīn & adab milestones — the gentle chart (never test him, only notice)

3 · Ḥifẓ & Qurʾān progress — Juz ʿAmma + the essentials

Method for a small child: 5 āyāt/day max, you recite → he repeats 3× → next day revise yesterday's first. Stop the moment he cries or refuses for 10 min straight; restart in 2 weeks. Never Qurʾān as punishment, never after a bad deed ("you sinned, now read"), never during sleep time. Reward = hug + date + a stamp here, not money.
