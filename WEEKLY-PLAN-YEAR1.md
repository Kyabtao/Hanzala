# Hanzala — 52 Weeks, One Islamic Year
### 5 September 2026 → 5 September 2027 (his age: 7 months → 19 months)
**Family style:** traditional / tasawwuf-leaning · **Languages at home:** English + Urdu/Hindi
Companion files: `TOY-AND-STUDY-PLAN.md` (toys & avoid-list by age) · `FRIDGE-CHECKLIST.md` (one-page tick list)

> **Rule of the year:** one āyah, one word, one sunnah habit, one toy, one walk — **each week**. Nothing more.
> A 19-month-old who says *Bismillāh*, *Allāh*, *duʿāʾ*, *āmīn*, *sajdah* and claps at the sound of the adhān has been taught more than a child pushed to memorise under pressure.

**Two calendar notes for you:**
- 12 Rabīʿ al-Awwal 1448 (South Asia) was **26 August 2026** — Mawlid has just passed. Don't chase it; the next is **Sunday 15 August 2027** = Week 50.
- All Hijri dates below are ±1 day. **Follow your local masjid's moon sighting**, especially for Ramaḍān and the two ʿĪds.

**One thing — the only thing that decides whether this works:** 20 minutes of you on the floor, phone in another room, five days a week. Not the toys.

## A. The fixed weekly frame (same shape all 52 weeks)
| Day | 20–30 min slot | Muslim content |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturday** | Water / sand / outdoor | ṣadaqah tin, feed the birds, "SubḥānAllāh" at what he sees |
| **Sunday** | Floor play + rhythm | ḥamd/naʿt with clapping and ḍuff only, dancing with Abū |
| **Monday** | Books + quiet | 1 board book + the week's āyah aloud |
| **Tuesday** | Skills | crawl/walk obstacle, cups, pincer (thumb-finger) games |
| **Wednesday** | **Raḥmah** | gentle with doll, cat, ants; share 2 biscuits; soft voice |
| **Thursday** | **Silaṭ ar-raḥim** | grandparents, phone call, plate to the neighbour |
| **Friday** | **Jumuʿah identity** | ghusl + thobe, masjid visit (he can play at the edge), sūrah al-Kahf in the house, special lunch, nails/hair not cut before he returns |

**Daily, no exceptions:** Bismillāh before food · Alḥamdulillāh after · 10 min Qurʾān audible · morning & evening adhkār · duʿāʾ using his name · **zero screens.**

## B. Week-by-week

### Month 1 — Rabīʿ al-Ākhir 1448 · Wks 1–4 · 5 Sep–4 Oct 2026 · **7 months**
**Theme: al-ḥamdu lillāh (शुक्र / شکر)** · **Ism of Allāh: al-Wāḥid الواحد**
| Wk | Dates | Āyah of the week | New words (AR / UR-HI / EN) | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5–11 Sep | al-Fātiḥah 1–2 | Allāh / اللہ / God | Sit him facing you; say "Allāh" 20×, one clap each time. Record it, play it at Ẓuhr. |
| 2 | 12–18 Sep | al-Fātiḥah 3–4 | alḥamdulillāh / الحمد لله | Every bite: "Bismillāh" before, "Alḥamdulillāh" finished. He watches your mouth — that's how he learns. |
| 3 | 19–25 Sep | Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ | ustad / استاد · teacher | Roll the ball to him; when it comes back: "Allāhu akbar!" Return = joy, the first social game. |
| 4 | 26 Sep–2 Oct | Sūrah al-Kawthar | pānī / पानी · water | **New toy: 3 hole-free bath cups.** Water play + the ḥadīth of the thirsty dog, in 3 sentences. |
**Check:** sits without support ~1 min · rolls both ways · laughs aloud · turns to his name
**Abū's task:** pray 2 rakʿah in the same room with him awake, 3× a week — he must *see* you bow.

### Month 2 — Jumādā al-Ūlā 1448 · Wks 5–8 · 3–30 Oct 2026 · **8–9 months**
**Theme: ʿaqīqah & his name** · **Ism: as-Salām السلام**
| Wk | Dates | Āyah | Words | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 3–9 Oct | Sūrah al-Falaq | ʿaqīqah / عقیقہ | If the 7th-day ʿaqīqah (2 sheep for a boy — sunnah) was missed, do it now, any day. Say his name at the duʿāʾ of the sacrifice; ⅓ to the poor. |
| 6 | 10–16 Oct | Sūrah an-Nās | kaun / कौन · who? | **New toy: soft fabric mirror.** "Kaun hai? — Ḥanzalā!" Point, name, smile. |
| 7 | 17–23 Oct | Sūrah al-Fīl | dāhāṇā / दाहिना · right | Right hand for eating: put food in his right hand every single time, say "dāhāṇā". |
| 8 | 24–30 Oct | Sūrah Quraysh | nīlā / नीला · blue | **New toy: 4 wooden rings / coloured rattles.** Name the colour + "SubḥānAllāh" each. |
**Check:** bears weight on legs · crawls or drags · pincer grasp · "mama/dada" babble · peek-a-boo understood
**Avoid:** Diwālī cracker toys and puja-style gift items. If relatives bring them, one kind sentence and into the drawer — no argument.

### Month 3 — Jumādā al-Ākhira 1448 · Nov 2026 · **9–10 months**
**Theme: raḥmah — mercy to every living thing** · **Ism: ar-Raḥīm الرحيم**
| Wk | Dates | Āyah | Words | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 31 Oct–6 Nov | Sūrah al-Lahab | jānvar / जानवर · animal | The cat and the dog stories (2 min). Put a crumb for the ants *with* his hand. |
| 10 | 7–13 Nov | Sūrah an-Naṣr | āmīn / آمین | Teach **āmīn**: you duʿā out loud "Allāhummaḥfaẓ Ḥanzalā" → he says "āmīn". The key skill of this month. |
| 11 | 14–20 Nov | Sūrah al-Kāfirūn | goḍiyā / गुड़िया · doll | **New toy: soft doll + blanket.** *You* stroke it first, then hand it to him: "naram, naram". |
| 12 | 21–27 Nov | Sūrah at-Takāthur | nahlānā / नहलाना · bath | Bath = splash + "Allāhu akbar". Let him wash his own face: ṭahārah as a happy habit, not a rule. |
**Check:** pulls to stand · waves bye-bye · puts a cube in a cup · knows "nahi" · claps
**Qurʾān at this age:** you recite, he hears. Loop al-Falaq, an-Nās, al-Ikhlāṣ at sleep. Do **not** attempt reading or flash-cards — at 10 months they teach nothing except that books are boring.

### Month 4 — Rajab 1448 · Dec 2026 · **11 months**
**Theme: the gift of ṣalāh made visible** · **Ism: al-Muḥyī المحيي**
| Wk | Dates | Āyah | Words | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | 28 Nov–4 Dec | Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 1 | namāz / नमाज़ | Lay *his* small rug next to yours. At your sujud, rest his forehead down once, then "MāshāAllāh!" Never force. |
| 14 | 5–11 Dec | Sūrah al-Muzzammil 1–10 | qayām | Once this month, wake 3 min before Fajr with him; hand on his head; duʿāʾ in Urdu/Hindi. |
| 15 | 12–18 Dec | Sūrah al-Qadr | rozā / रोज़ा | Say the word now so it's ordinary by Ramaḍān: "Allāh kā rozā" — and when you drink, "Alḥamdulillāh". |
| 16 | 19–25 Dec | Sūrah al-Fātiḥah (review) | qurṭā / कुर्ता | **New toy: 6 stacking cups + push-trolley** (not a walker). No Christmas tree/snowman toys — take gifts with a smile, keep in the cupboard. |
| 17 | 26 Dec–1 Jan | Sūrah al-Muzzammil (review) | salām / सलाम | **1 Jan:** no party. Night of ḥisāb — 2 rakʿah shukr, Sūrah al-Mulk, write his duʿāʾ for the coming year. (The *sunnah* year starts in June — Week 42.) |
**Check:** cruises along furniture · first word likely · imitates gestures · finds a hidden toy
**27 Rajab ≈ 5 Jan 2027 (Shab-e-Merāj):** nothing special for an infant. The real benefit of Rajab is that your ṣalāh is now visible to him daily — that's the sīrah lesson.

### Month 5 — Shaʿbān 1448 · Jan 2027 · **12 months + birthday 11 Jan**
**Theme: shukr for one year, and parents' duʿāʾ** · **Ism: ash-Shakūr الشكور**
| Wk | Dates | Āyah | Words | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | 2–8 Jan | al-Fātiḥah (full) | Ummī / أمي · mom | "Ummī, Ummī" games ×10 daily. The day she is thanked by him will be his ṣalāh. |
| 19 | **9–15 Jan — 11 Jan: 1 year** | Sūrah aḍ-Ḍuḥā | shukrānā | **No cake-party — a shukr day:** you fast; feed one family one meal; write the *duʿāʾ letter* ("Yā Allāh, make Ḥanzalā a ḥāfiẓ of Your book…"); recite aḍ-Ḍuḥā: *"wa ammā bi-niʿmati rabbika fa-ḥaddith."* Print one photo, file it under his name. |
| 20 | 16–22 Jan | Sūrah ash-Sharḥ | bhookh / भूख | Two cups, two biscuits: "ek tum, ek Ummī". Sharing practised now = no greed lecture at 8. |
| 21 | 23–29 Jan | Sūrah at-Tīn | dān / दान · give | **Shab-e-Barat ≈ 24 Jan:** no ritual for the baby; you pray 2 rakʿah and add his name to your night duʿāʾ. Teach later: we ask *only* Allāh. |
| 22 | 30 Jan–5 Feb | Sūrah al-ʿAlaq 1–5 | iqrā / इक़रा · read | **New toy: chunky crayons + paper taped to the wall.** Free scribble; you name what his scribble "is". |
**Check:** stands alone a few seconds · 1 real word · points with index finger · walks holding one hand · drinks from an open cup
**Ramaḍān starts Mon 8 Feb — prep this week:** dates jar, his own tiny plate, and tell the family he "joins" suḥūr and iftār without fasting.

### Month 6 — RAMAḌĀN 1448 · 8 Feb – 9 Mar 2027 · **13 months — the best month of the year**
**Theme: the house becomes a madrasah** · **Ism: al-Ghaffūr الغفور**
| Wk | Dates | Āyah | Words | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | 30 Jan–6 Feb (Ramaḍān from Mon 8 Feb) | Sūrah al-Baqarah 183 (you recite) | Ramaḍān / रमज़ान | First suḥūr: wake him 10 min, one date on the tongue, water, "Bismillāh", "Allāhu akbar", back to sleep. This one memory is worth a hundred toys. |
| 24 | 7–13 Feb | Sūrah an-Naba | ifṭār / इफ़्तार | His job: hand you the date and the glass. "JazākAllāhu khayran" — teach the thank-you of the dīn. |
| 25 | 14–20 Feb | Sūrah ash-Shams | duʿā / दुआ | After the Maghrib adhān he says "āmīn" with you. Tarāwīḥ: 15 min at the masjid, playing at the edge — that counts. |
| 26 | 21 Feb–6 Mar (odd nights, 27th ≈ Sat 6 Mar) | Sūrah al-Inshirāḥ | ṣadaqah / दान | Odd nights, esp. **27th ≈ Sat 6 Mar:** put his hand in the ṣadaqah box so he "gives". Quiet house, low lights, early sleep — no all-night show. |
| 27 | 6–12 Mar | Sūrah al-Bayyinah | zakāh / ज़कात | **New toy: play food + mini broom.** He "helps" clean: the fiqh of service enters through the hands. |
| 28 | **ʿĪd al-Fiṭr ≈ 9–10 Mar** | Sūrah al-Aʿlā | ʿīd / ईद | Ghusl + new clothes; Zakāh al-Fiṭr paid in his name — say "Ḥanzalā kī taraf se, Allāh qabūl farmāye"; sevāīyā on the tongue; neighbour visits; one photo for the "ʿĪd wall". |
**Ramaḍān rules for a 13-month-old:** no screens (kids' channels are full of music and junk), no extra sugar, keep his sleep routine, don't wake him every night, and no adult "tricks" that make fasting look like punishment.
**His Ramaḍān word target (3 words only):** *Allāh · duʿā · āmīn.* In a Hindi-speaking home keep Islamic terms in Arabic/Urdu — say **Allāh**, not a translated devotional word, so from the start the name of tawḥīd is unmistakable.

## C. Year-1 word bank — 24 words, three languages each
`Allāh · Ummī · Abū · Bismillāh · Alḥamdulillāh · Allāhu akbar · SubḥānAllāh · āmīn · duʿā · namāz/salah · sajdah · adhān · ʿīd · Ramaḍān · rozā · ifṭār · ṣadaqah · Kaʿbah · pānī/water · dāhāṇā/right · sonā/sleep · khānā/food · jānvar/animal · kitāb/book · phool/flower`
Say them **while doing the thing**, never as a test. 5 repetitions a day, 1 week per word, is enough at this age.
*(Hindi/Urdu mix note: keep the dīn vocabulary in Arabic/Urdu — Allāh, namāz, roza, duʿā, āmīn. That is how the subcontinent kept ʿaqīdah clear for centuries, and it's easier for a child than two competing religious vocabularies.)*

## D. Duʿāʾ set for the 52 weeks
1. Before food: *Bismillāh* · if forgotten: *Bismillāhi fī kulli waḥtin wa ʿalā ḥālin*
2. After food: *Alḥamdulillāhi lladhī aṭʿamanā wa saqānā wa jaʿalanā muslimīn*
3. Sleep: *Bismik Allāhumma amūtu wa aḥyā* + 3 Qul + Āyat al-Kursī, blow into your hands, wipe over his body (the sunnah for small children)
4. **The Prophet's ﷺ duʿāʾ for al-Ḥasan & al-Ḥusayn — on his head, every morning and every evening, all 52 weeks:**
   *Uʿīdhukā kalimāti Llāhi t-tāmmati min kulli shayṭānin wa hāmmatin, wa min kulli ʿaynin lāmmah.*
5. Morning: *Aṣbaḥnā wa aṣbaḥa l-mulku lillāh* · Evening: *Amsaynā wa amsā l-mulku lillāh*
6. Your own, in Urdu/Hindi — say it aloud near him often:
   *"Allāh, Ḥanzalā ko ḥifẓ me rakh. Usko dīn aur duniyā me samajh de. Har buraī se bachā. Āmīn."*

## E. Sunday-night review — 2 minutes, one line in the diary
```
Week __ | Āyah: ______ | Words he said: ______ | What was too much: ______ | Duʿāʾ for him by name: ☐ every day
```
If a week fails: **halve it**, don't abandon it. When you are exhausted, do the one thing left standing — duʿāʾ for him before Fajr. It is the heaviest item in this plan and the cheapest.
